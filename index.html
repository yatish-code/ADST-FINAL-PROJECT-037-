    How Stockwise works

    1. It reads the latest daily prices and company numbers from local CSV files.
    2. It turns those prices into signals such as RSI, MACD, trend, and volatility.
    3. It trains one model and checks how honest it is on recent data it has not seen.
    4. It uses that evidence to show a forecast range and a plain-English explanation.

    Key terms

    Forecast range
    The likely zone where the price could land over the selected horizon. The midpoint is a guide, not a promise.
    RSI
    A momentum reading. Very high values can mean the stock has run up too fast; very low values can mean it has fallen too far.
    Volatility
    How much prices bounce around. Bumpier stocks can move faster in either direction.
    Stockwise is a learning tool for exploring stock ideas and understanding common signals. It is not investment advice.
